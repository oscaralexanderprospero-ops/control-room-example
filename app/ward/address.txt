## The Charter

This is the charter every agent reads first. It is served verbatim by the app at /api/charter.

Replace this file with your own. A good charter says, in plain words:

- what the agents may do on your behalf;
- what they must never do (publish without your click, write words you did not say, open private folders);
- how they should talk to you.

##

Nothing in this example charter is a real person's. It is a placeholder.
